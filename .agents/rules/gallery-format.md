# Gallery Formatting Rules

- **Minimal & Standardized Gallery Format**:
  - Do NOT add redundant section headings, promotional labels, or meta badges above or around the gallery (e.g., avoid "PHOTO GALLERY", "全10点（エクステリア5点、インテリア5点）", "公式プレスキットギャラリー").
  - Do NOT prepend category prefixes or tags to individual photo titles or captions (e.g., avoid "【エクステリア】", "【インテリア】").
  - Keep slide metadata strictly minimal: each item only carries the essential clean title/caption describing the visual subject.
  - The gallery should render cleanly as a pure visual component (images, counter indicator, navigation controls, and concise description), without editorial clutter.
